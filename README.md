A one-star review of Earth, written by an alien tourist who still admits the food was excellent — in exactly one sentence.

## Entry

> Earth's gravity flattened my knees, the queue outlasted my planet's entire orbit, and the rain fell sideways, yet the noodles were so good I would crash-land here again just to eat them.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program is fully deterministic (no randomness, no clock) and writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (one sentence, 1–200 characters).
